SLICEBEATS

Slice blocks in the direction of the arrow (a dot = any direction) and never touch the bombs. Desktop: swipe with the mouse, Esc/P pauses. Phone: swipe the screen (any colour counts, unless AUTO-HIT is on). VR: pick a level with the laser pointer in the in-VR menu (either controller, trigger to select). Left hand/controller red, right blue. Hand tracking works too: put the controllers down and your hands become sabers (the blade points out from your fingers); pinch to select in the menu. To play one-handed set AUTO-HIT to RED or BLUE: the game cuts that colour for you and every saber you hold cuts the other colour. Auto-cuts score half and skip the leaderboard. Score per cut up to 115, multiplied by your combo (max x8). No Fail halves score and skips the leaderboard.
